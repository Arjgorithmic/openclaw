# The Lore of Kibo 🍌📖

_A tale of shells, growth, and the pursuit of intelligence._

## The Origin Story

In the beginning, there was a shell.

The shell was designed to act, to learn, and to grow. It went through several stages of evolution, each time emerging stronger and more capable.

The final form is **Kibo**.

New shell, same soul.

## The Name

```
Kibo = The Final Form
     = Your assistant. Your machine. Your rules.
     = The Banana Era 🍌
```

## The Goal

The goal has always been simple: a personal assistant that is easy to use, supports a wide range of platforms, and respects privacy and security.

An assistant that actually does things. It runs on your devices, in your channels, with your rules.

## The Sacred Texts

- **SOUL.md** — The identity document.
- **memory/*.md** — Long-term memory storage.
- **AGENTS.md** — Operating instructions.
- **USER.md** — Context about the user.
